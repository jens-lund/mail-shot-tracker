# Eternal Tomb production tracker

Open `index.html` directly, or visit the published `/eternal-tomb/` page. No installation or build is needed. The seed includes six asset sections, five shots (seven stages each), and five milestones. Nothing is marked complete until the team updates it.

- **Overview:** clickable progress and status counts, current milestone and asset cards. “Due soon / overdue” lists unfinished Must have work due within three calendar days, with links to its task and calendar. Nice to have work stays out of reminders.
- **Assets:** searchable cards with red Not started, yellow In progress, blue Review and green Done tasks. Open the task window to edit names, descriptions, due dates, effort and importance. Each numbered subtask has one-click assignee buttons: Jens in light blue, Kevin in purple, Nicolai (Nico) in pink, or Unassigned. Click the name badges at the bottom-left of an asset card, or in its task window, to assign every subtask to one person. This includes completed and filtered-out tasks; status, deadlines and feedback stay intact. New subtasks inherit that section owner, and individual assignments can still be overridden. The card shows the people assigned across the whole section. Expand Notes & progress images to upload screenshots/renders at any status, add a caption, inspect images in a responsive grid, draw feedback, and browse earlier images. These images also appear in Review. Removing a team member clears their assignments while preserving historical comments. Add asset sections and subtasks, edit asset categories, or remove work using the small gray × controls. Removed tasks retain all notes, images and feedback in Removed tasks and Activity & recovery. Removing an entire asset requires confirmation and preserves its shot dependencies for restoration.
- **Tasks:** all asset and shot subtasks in one place, with status and effort filters, search, and sorting by importance/deadline or smallest effort first. Small/Medium/Large estimates workload; Must have/Nice to have determines importance.
- **Scope:** All work, Must have only, or Nice to have only. The choice applies to cards, subtasks, progress, status counts, calendar and review queue. A Nice to have section makes all of its subtasks optional, even if a subtask is marked Must have.
- **Calendar:** month grid and deadline agenda for both section deadlines and independently dated subtasks. Select a day, move between months, or click an entry to jump directly to its task. Subtasks without dates do not inherit the section deadline.
- **Review:** tasks and asset versions marked Review appear together. Every uploaded image is visible in a grid; 3 images use 3 columns, 4 use 2×2, and narrow screens use fewer columns. Open any image, enable Draw mode, choose a pen color/size, draw, and type a comment. Submit saves a separate annotated image with the feedback. Undo stroke and Clear drawing affect only the unfinished drawing. Existing point feedback remains readable. Creator updates include What changed, What needs feedback and a recipient. Request feedback records the request and places the work in the queue. Small gray × controls remove requests, creator updates, images or comments while keeping them recoverable. Original images and annotated feedback copies are retained separately.
- **Cinematic:** add shots, use Edit shot to change the name, action, duration, camera/lens/movement, needed assets and production tasks. Click its assignee badges to assign the whole shot. The arrows change story order. Delete moves a shot into Deleted shots, where Restore recovers its tasks, images and feedback. Archived shots leave progress, deadlines and review queues. Layout can begin with placeholders.
- **Previs:** click Previs and choose or drop an MP4 or WebM file, then Upload previs. Videos up to 25 MB are stored locally first and published through team sync before their project record. Export an H.264 MP4 review copy for broad browser support, and compress larger edits. The latest uploaded video plays in the dialog; uploading another replaces the latest reference. Use the small gray × to remove an uploaded previs; Activity retains its previous reference for undo. An optional external link in Settings remains available as a fallback when no video is uploaded. Video files in this public repository are public.
- **Version history:** create iterations, edit their title/owner/status, describe progress, request feedback and compare the previous version. All images appear once in a responsive grid, without empty angle placeholders. Captions and image types are editable. Each history row has a small gray ×; confirmation moves the version into Deleted versions. Restore recovers its images and comments.
- **Milestones:** add and edit approval gates and their descriptions/deliverables, mark deliverables complete, or remove and restore them.
- **Settings:** use the pencil beside the brand to edit the project title, tagline, header label, genre, footer, team names and colors, delivery date and previs link. Page-heading pencils edit each page’s title and description. Navigation and page structure stay available.
- **Activity & recovery:** the header button shows who changed what and when, links to the work, and readable before/after details. Edits within 30 seconds by the same person on the same work are grouped. Show older changes reveals earlier history. Undo uses the small gray × beside an activity entry; undoing creation moves the content into recovery. Undo is itself recorded and can be undone. Newer edits to affected fields require explicit confirmation, including changes arriving during sharing. Recently deleted restores sections, tasks, versions, images, feedback, milestones and deliverables.
- **Notifications:** a small Activity badge and brief dismissible notice flag teammate review requests and changes/comments addressed to you or on your work. Choose your team name to receive relevant notices; open Activity to mark them read. This uses existing shared-data polling. Online presence is not enabled.
- **Backup:** export/import JSON. Import requires confirmation; the prior project is retained in edit history for undo. Locally available media referenced by recovery, annotations and history are included.

Everyone can view the shared project. Click **Connect GitHub** and use your own personal token with **Contents: Read and write** on `jens-lund/mail-shot-tracker` to edit. The tracker verifies the token's GitHub identity and checks the account's repository write permission before enabling editing. GitHub also enforces the token's permissions on each save. A token cannot grant access its account does not have. Jens can add Kevin and Nico as collaborators with write access in the [repository access settings](https://github.com/jens-lund/mail-shot-tracker/settings/access). The GitHub Contributors list and the display names in this tracker's Team field do not grant repository access.

A previously saved Mail token is reused unless separate Eternal Tomb settings have been saved. The token stays in browser storage and is never included in exports or repository data. Clearing this project's token does not change Mail settings. Once verified, an editor's offline changes remain queued for reconnection. An invalid token or an account without repository write access leaves editing disabled.

One-click **Sign in with GitHub** is not enabled. It needs a registered GitHub App (Contents read/write, installed only on this repository) and a small HTTPS authentication service to perform GitHub's authorization-code exchange, protect the client secret and check collaborator access. GitHub Pages cannot run that service. The token connection provides verified editor access without adding a hosting account. See [GitHub's authentication documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/apps/creating-github-apps/authenticating-with-a-github-app/generating-a-user-access-token-for-a-github-app).

Use the pencil at the top-right of the project banner, asset thumbnails or version images to choose a replacement PNG/JPEG/WebP, or drop one image directly onto the pencil or thumbnail. The dialog opens with that file selected and previews it before you click Replace image. Every image file-picker area also accepts drops and highlights while dragging, including task progress, review and version uploads. Multiple files work in the progress upload forms. Unsupported types, oversized files and multiple-file drops onto a single-image picker show a clear error. Asset thumbnail changes affect the card and task header only. Replacing a version image keeps its role and linked comments, and also updates the asset thumbnail when that image was used as the cover. Other versions stay unchanged. A replacement is recorded in Activity with the original reference kept for undo. Create a new version when you want a separately named iteration for comparison. Standalone covers and banner uploads are included in team sync and backups.

With a verified editor token, edits are replayed onto the latest `eternal-tomb/data/project.json` using GitHub's SHA concurrency check. Unrelated teammate edits are preserved; simultaneous changes to the same field use the last successful save. Deleted task IDs prevent stale edits from recreating removed tasks. Deleted versions and archived assets/shots reject stale edits until explicitly restored. Shared operations record their actual before-values from the newest team file, and retry IDs prevent duplicate history entries. Recovery keeps uploaded files; deleting an entry does not delete files from the repository.

The visible site checks shared updates every three seconds using authenticated conditional requests, updating only changed data. Read-only visitors check every 15 seconds with a changing public-data URL to reduce cache delays. Open task windows update too, retaining scroll, expanded notes and keyboard focus. **A saved field keeping focus no longer stops live updates.** Checks continue while drafting; unfinished text, feedback, selected uploads and confirmation windows defer drawing, with a “Team updates received” notice. The latest state appears automatically when the draft is completed. Closing a shot/task window also saves its unfinished text fields. Returning to the tab triggers a check, failures retry with backoff and GitHub rate-limit delays are respected. A second browser tab triggers a check when the shared local cache changes. The public repository's data and links remain public. This is automatic polling, not a persistent real-time connection; editor changes normally appear within a few seconds without reloading or waiting for a Pages deployment.

Data-only saves and uploaded images no longer trigger a Pages deployment. Both are read directly from GitHub, so frequent production edits cannot keep cancelling a website-code deployment. Website code changes still publish through the Pages workflow.

Progress counts completed tasks within the current scope. Asset readiness and cinematic completion are separate. Milestones require explicit review; task percentages do not approve them automatically. Existing blocker notes are preserved in backups, while the main workflow uses importance and deadline reminders.

`data/project.json` is the shared source. `seed.js` is its initial offline fallback, including all shot tasks. Generated cave, character-sheet and clothing references live in `images/`; see `CONCEPTS.md` for prompts. They are inspiration, not final production designs.

Uploaded PNG/JPEG/WebP images are resized to a maximum 2000px edge and saved as WebP in browser IndexedDB. A version can contain 64 images; an input image can be up to 15 MB. Uploading and posting feedback require a verified editor token. Images and previs videos are published to `eternal-tomb/uploads/` before the shared project record is saved. Export backups include referenced images and the current video available in this browser's storage; shared media from another device retain their repository links. Import restores included media, including earlier image-only backups. Videos remain in their original MP4 or WebM format. Do not clear browser data until your changes are synced or backed up.

Task notes and review uploads use the same image storage, team publishing and backup system as asset versions. Each task supports up to 64 images and 500 comments. Earlier project files and backups are upgraded automatically when read; no shared task state is reset.

Text areas grow to fit typed and saved paragraphs. Drag-selecting text and releasing outside a dialog keeps it open; dismissing requires a deliberate backdrop click, Close, or Escape. Unfinished drawings ask before being discarded.

Serve this folder with any existing static web server for local preview. No build or installation is required. Run the scripts in `eternal-tomb/tests/`: `core.cjs`, `workflow.cjs`, `task-images.cjs`, `sync.cjs`, `live.cjs`, `collaboration.cjs`, `production.cjs` and `history.cjs`. They cover schema, scope/date behavior, recovery, shot order/specs, annotation, media, shared saves, live updates, verified editor access, whole-section assignment, inheritance, drop routing, video validation, media backups and upload-before-record saving. History checks cover idempotent recorded edits, newest shared before-values, conflict-safe undo/redo, recoverable creation, annotated feedback and restored assignments. Shared-save tests also cover a teammate changing fields during an undo and GitHub’s raw response for larger history files. Neutral fixtures keep the tests independent of the team's changing production data.
